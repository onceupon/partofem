Bubble Support

Bubble: Pop Your To-Dos

Need a hand, found a bug, or have an idea? We'd love to hear from you.

  • In the app: while signed in, type #feedback followed by your message, and it comes straight to us.
  • On Instagram: message @poem_partofem.

We're a tiny team, so replies can take a few days.

Getting started

Type a task and press Enter to blow a bubble. Pop it when you're done. Open the How it works panel any time for tips and magic tags.

New here? The illustrated cheat sheet shows everything Bubble can do on one page.

Do I need an account?

No. Bubble works fully without one, and your tasks stay on your device. Sign in with Apple, Google, or email only if you'd like your bubbles to sync between your phone, tablet, and computer.

Using Bubble on a computer

Your bubbles are on the web too, at bubble.partofem.com. Sign in with the same account to see the same board.

My bubbles didn't sync

Make sure you're signed in with the same account on each device, and that you're online. Bubble fetches the latest board whenever you come back to the app or get back online.

Keeping a copy

Bubble is a small hobby app, so please don't keep anything important only here. You can export your full task history as a CSV from the How it works panel.

Deleting your account

Open the Account panel and tap Delete account. Your account and all synced tasks are removed for good. See the Privacy Policy for details.
